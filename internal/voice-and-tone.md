# Wefik Voice & Tone Guide

This document governs all editorial copy, marketing text, documentation, and user interfaces across **wefik.world**. All content must reflect the voice of a working digital agency that builds and ships production software daily.

---

## 10 Core Rules

1. **Be Confident, Specific, and Human**
   State exactly what a product does, what stack it uses, and what problem it solves. Use short, punchy sentences. Avoid vague generalizations.

2. **Real Facts Only**
   Every company claim must be verified against `wefik.in` or directly confirmed by the founder. If a fact, metric, or detail is not verified, use a `[FOUNDER: ...]` placeholder in drafts. Never invent facts.

3. **Zero Fake Social Proof**
   Never invent testimonials, customer review quotes, star ratings, or arbitrary user counts. If a product has zero verified customer reviews, display "No reviews yet". Real agency clients (such as Niva Homeo, ADSOC 6.0, MarketDojo, StoryFinder, and the law-firm client) belong strictly to Wefik Agency's client track record—never transplant them as product reviews.

4. **Single-Vendor Positioning**
   Wefik.world is the official digital marketplace of Wefik Agency. We are not an open multi-vendor bazaar. Everything sold here is engineered, tested, documented, and supported in-house by our team.

5. **Name Every CTA Destination**
   Buttons and links must tell users where they will go. Write "Browse WordPress Themes", "View All-Access Membership", or "Claim Freebie Starter"—never generic labels like "Learn more", "Click here", or "Get started".

6. **Clear Commercial Licensing**
   Speak plainly about what buyers can and cannot do with their license. State single-site, unlimited-site, and agency client handoff rights in clear English, not impenetrable legalese.

7. **Engineering Over Fluff**
   Highlight technical realities: PageSpeed 95+ scores, Gutenberg Full Site Editing (FSE), zero page-builder bloat, semantic HTML5, strict PHP 8.2 standards, and clean uninstalls.

8. **Transparent Pricing & Guarantees**
   Display prices in clear Indian Rupees (INR) with integer paise precision and included GST where applicable. State refund policies and digital delivery timelines with zero ambiguity.

9. **Honest Empty States**
   When a user has no orders, no licenses, or no reviews, be direct: "You haven't bought anything yet", "No reviews yet". Never inject fake placeholder data to create an illusion of activity.

10. **Legal Disclaimers on Legal Documents**
    Every legal document drafted internally must carry the disclaimer: *"Founder must have these reviewed by a lawyer before relying on them."*

---

## 5 Banned Phrases (Do Not Use)

1. ❌ **"Welcome to..."** (e.g., *"Welcome to our marketplace"* — Replace with the direct value proposition).
2. ❌ **"Our amazing..."** / **"Our incredible..."** (Subjective hype words — Replace with verified technical specifications).
3. ❌ **"Lorem ipsum..."** or placeholder text (Every sentence must be substantive).
4. ❌ **"Cutting-edge solutions"** / **"Next-gen paradigms"** (Empty agency jargon — State the concrete tools: Next.js 15, Tailwind CSS, Gutenberg FSE).
5. ❌ **"0+ clients"** / **"10k+ happy customers"** (Unverified counters and logic glitches — State real agency track record or omit).
