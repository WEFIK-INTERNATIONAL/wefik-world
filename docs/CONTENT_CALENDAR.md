## Editorial Guardrails for Every Post
1. **Word Count:** 800–1,500 words for cluster posts; 3,000+ words for pillar guides.
2. **Internal Links:** Every post must link UP to its category hub (`/wordpress-themes` or `/wordpress-plugins` or `/html-templates`), SIDEWAYS to 2 sibling cluster posts, and DOWN to 1–2 specific product pages.
3. **Structured Data:** Every post renders valid `BlogPosting` JSON-LD schema with author credentials and `FAQPage` schema for the 2–4 included FAQ questions.
4. **Keyword Cannibalization Protection:** Each URL is registered in `keyword-map.csv` before publishing.
